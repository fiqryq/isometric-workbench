What we heard

The brief

A macOS app that turns simple parts into clean, animated isometric blueprints: exploded views, section cuts and labelled diagrams. For designers, technical illustrators and makers. The mark should feel precise and crafted, read at 16 px, and avoid the generic cube-and-grid look of the category.

precisecraftedclearquietly technical

Success: says 'exploded view' at a glance, distinct from plain isometric cubes, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour.

Final design

Lift

The top of the block lifts off: an exploded view in its simplest form.

  • Shows the app's signature move, exploding a part, in three flat shapes
  • The moving part is marked in Redline, the body in Blueprint blue: both palettes ship in the app
  • Built on the same 30° isometric grid the app draws on, so it sits naturally on the canvas
  • A small-size cut opens the gaps so the lift still reads at 16 px
